\subsection{Artifact Provenance and Claim Status}
\label{app:provenance}

The source manifest retains complete paths and digests. Its SHA-256 prefixes
are listed separately to preserve readable two-column typesetting:
\begin{itemize}
  \item dataset: \texttt{991cb3810e88};
  \item complete-test aggregate: \texttt{af97717abbd5};
  \item per-target replay table: \texttt{716bb6f426c4};
  \item paired qualitative inverse figure: \texttt{62830e47306e}.
\end{itemize}

The forward evaluation is formally completed over three seeds. The inverse
evaluation is a \emph{single-seed complete-test replay}: it covers all 625
held-out targets at seed 0. This protocol does not assess cross-seed inverse
stability, universal architectural superiority, PINO/field inverse gains, or
causal differences from an unmatched historical seed-2 run.

\section{Additional Interpretation}

The single-mode generator's MPB MRE interval is [2.2263, 2.2965]\%; the
four-mode interval is [2.3485, 2.4341]\%. The paired interval controls for
target-specific difficulty because both methods evaluate the same targets.
However, it remains conditional on one trained pair of inverse models. A
multi-seed follow-up should repeat the objective-bridge training and replay
protocol while preserving the target pool and MPB budget.
