\section{Problem Formulation}

Let \(\design\in\{0,1\}^{H\times W}\) denote a binary material layout on a
square periodic unit cell. The dataset enforces \(\pgroup\) symmetry, and the
permittivity is
\begin{equation}
  \permittivity(\mathbf{x})=\epsilon_{\mathrm{air}}+
  (\epsilon_{\mathrm{mat}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{air}})\design(\mathbf{x}).
  \label{eq:permittivity}
\end{equation}
For sampled Bloch vectors
\(\kpath=\{\mathbf{k}_i\}_{i=1}^{N_k}\), an MPB eigensolve defines the
forward map
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{F}^{\star}:\permittivity\longmapsto
  \mathbf{W}=[\omega_{i,b}]\in\mathbb{R}^{N_k\times N_b},
  \label{eq:physical-forward}
\end{equation}
where frequencies are normalized by \(a/(2\pi c)\). BISON learns
\(\bandop\approx\mathcal{F}^{\star}\) from paired structures and band
matrices. The implementation obtains \(H,W,N_k,N_b\) from the data schema. The
reported dataset instance has \(H=W=16\), \(N_k=31\), and \(N_b=10\).

Joint prediction of \(\mathbf{W}\) preserves the output structure required for
inverse design. A target may specify a local gap, band slope, or multi-band
profile within one ordered eigenspectrum. Independent scalar predictions would
not explicitly model correlations across neighboring wave vectors and bands.
BISON therefore produces the full \(N_k\times N_b\) matrix in one pass. The
frozen inverse metric excludes band 1 for the numerical reason given below.

Given a target \(\bandtarget\), inverse design seeks one or more legal
structures whose solver response matches that target. A latent conditional
generator produces
\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\design}_{j}=\generator(\bandtarget,\mathbf{z}_j),
  \qquad \mathbf{z}_j\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I}),
  \quad j=1,\ldots,K.
  \label{eq:inverse-generation}
\end{equation}
Multiple structures can realize similar spectra, so recovering the paired
training geometry is not the objective. We evaluate spectral realization
instead. For bands \(\mathcal{B}=\{2,\ldots,N_b\}\), the per-target mean
relative error is
\begin{equation}
  \mre(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B})=
  \frac{100}{N_k|\mathcal{B}|}
  \sum_{i=1}^{N_k}\sum_{b\in\mathcal{B}}
  \frac{|A_{i,b}-B_{i,b}|}{|B_{i,b}|}.
  \label{eq:mre}
\end{equation}
Band 1 is excluded by the frozen protocol because its near-zero values make a
relative metric ill-conditioned.

The inverse problem is a constrained selection task, not the estimation of a
unique \(\mathcal{F}^{\star-1}\). The workflow samples legal geometries, ranks
them with learned surrogates, and assigns a limited solver budget to distinct,
high-ranked candidates. Candidate count \(K\), deduplication, and replay count
are therefore part of the experimental protocol.

The evaluation has three distinct evidence levels. A
\emph{surrogate-only} value compares \(\bandop(\widehat{\design})\) with the
target. A \emph{cross-surrogate audit} additionally compares a second model
queried at physical \(\mathbf{k}\) coordinates. A \emph{solver-replayed}
value evaluates \(\mpb(\widehat{\design})\). BISON guides search, the query
operator diagnoses cross-surrogate disagreement, and MPB supplies the reported
physical response.
