\section{Method}

\subsection{Band-Informed Spectral Operator}

BISON takes \(\permittivity\) as a one-channel periodic field. A
\(1\times1\) convolution lifts it to \(C\) latent channels,
\(\mathbf{u}_0=P\permittivity\), followed by four BandBlocks. In block
\(\ell\), the spectral path is
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{s}_{\ell}=\mathcal{F}^{-1}\!\left[
  \left(R^{\mathrm{full}}_{\ell}+R^{\mathrm{band}}_{\ell}\right)
  \mathcal{F}(\mathbf{u}_{\ell})\right],
  \label{eq:dual-spectral}
\end{equation}
where two independently learned complex kernels act on configured
low-frequency rectangles. The band kernel uses a separately configured subset,
and the kernels are added in Fourier space. A pointwise convolution supplies a
local path, and a sigmoid gate modulates their sum:
\begin{align}
  \mathbf{a}_{\ell}&=\mathbf{s}_{\ell}+W_{\ell}\mathbf{u}_{\ell},\\
  \widetilde{\mathbf{a}}_{\ell}
  &=\sigma(G_{\ell}\mathbf{a}_{\ell})\odot\mathbf{a}_{\ell}.
\end{align}
A low-rank branch globally pools the block input, applies
\(C\rightarrow r\rightarrow C\) channel maps, and broadcasts the resulting
context vector. The block output is
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{u}_{\ell+1}=\operatorname{GELU}\!\left(
  S_{\ell}[\widetilde{\mathbf{a}}_{\ell}+L_{\ell}(\mathbf{u}_{\ell})]
  +\mathbf{u}_{\ell}\right).
  \label{eq:bandblock}
\end{equation}
Adaptive global pooling and a linear head map the final channels to
\(\bandpred\in\mathbb{R}^{N_k\times N_b}\). In the evaluated configuration,
\(C=32\), \(r=8\), and the spectral rectangles retain \(8\times8\) and
\(5\times5\) modes. The four-block network contains 389,718 trainable
parameters. Both Fourier weights are complex-valued and are cropped at runtime
when the grid provides fewer modes.

Figure~\ref{fig:bison-detailed} shows the network-level flow and the internal
BandBlock update. The gate acts on the combined spectral and pointwise paths,
whereas the low-rank context is computed from the block input.

BISON is an FNO-inspired spectral surrogate specialized for joint full-band
prediction. Fourier mixing models global interactions in the periodic field,
while the second kernel provides a separate learnable low-mode scale. The gate
controls the mixed channels, and the pooled bottleneck supplies cell-wide
context. All components are learned from paired permittivity--band data; no
Maxwell residual is included in training.

\subsection{Independent Query-Operator Audit}

We separately train \(\queryop(\permittivity,\mathbf{k})\) to predict the band
vector at a physical Bloch coordinate. Evaluation covers all sampled queries,
withheld interpolation points, and a contiguous held-out path segment. During
inverse evaluation, BISON remains the objective and primary ranker;
\(\queryop\) records an independent consistency diagnostic. It neither reranks
candidates nor replaces MPB replay.

\subsection{Single-Mode Routing-Free Candidate Generator}

The generator flattens the target matrix and encodes it with two
GELU-activated linear layers. It concatenates this representation with a
32-dimensional Gaussian latent vector and one fixed mode embedding. A
four-layer, width-256 MLP then produces a sigmoid output. With one fixed mode,
all samples share the same parameters and vary only through the continuous
latent vector. The output contains the independent degrees of freedom of the
symmetric binary design. Deterministic lifting and thresholding reconstruct
the complete \(\pgroup\) unit cell.

Paired reconstruction provides the warm start. The inverse model is then
optimized for 50 epochs with AdamW while BISON and the query operator remain
frozen. For 16 latent candidates per training condition, the objective is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
  \mathcal{L}={}&\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BISON}}
  +0.03\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rec}}
  +0.20\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{div}}
  +0.50\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bin\text{-}div}}\\
  &+0.25\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{latent}}
  +0.90\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{collision}}
  +0.20\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cross}}
  +0.20\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gap}}.
  \label{eq:inverse-objective}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The loss terms control target fidelity and warm-start reconstruction. They also
regulate continuous and binary diversity, latent responsiveness, near-duplicate
collisions, and cross-surrogate agreement. MPB gradients are not used. BISON defines the
learned full-band objective, whereas the generator proposes legal candidates.
The comparison method uses the same decoder with four cyclic mode embeddings;
it has no learned router or independent experts.

\subsection{Candidate Ranking and Solver Replay}

At inference, each target receives \(K=128\) latent draws. Candidates are
thresholded at 0.5, checked for legality, and deduplicated with a normalized
Hamming threshold defined by the geometry degrees of freedom. Frozen BISON
ranks the remaining candidates by bands-2--10 MRE. The query model records a
separate diagnostic score but does not alter the ranking. MPB recomputes the
five highest-ranked candidates, and evaluation reports the lowest real-MPB
MRE. The four-mode embedding variant serves only as a same-budget baseline.
